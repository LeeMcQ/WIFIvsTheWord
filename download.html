Honest questions

Before you download

Is it really free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads and nothing to unlock. It's given away as part of the ministry. If it blesses your home, you can give a small gift to help reach another family, but you never have to.

Will it track me?

No. There are no ad networks or tracking SDKs. Your screen-time figures, journal, and confessions stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Does it need the internet?

No. The whole Bible is installed inside the app, so reading, the Sabbath countdown, prayer, and the journal all work offline — even in airplane mode. The only feature that uses a connection is the optional companion chat.

Why a .apk — isn't it on the Play Store?

We're rolling out on Google Play; while that's in review you can install the app directly with the file above. It's the same app, signed by the ministry. iPhone follows once Apple's review clears.

Is it only for Seventh-day Adventists?

It was born in an Adventist context, but the heart of it — guarding the mind, restoring the family, refocusing on Christ — is for every Christian home.
