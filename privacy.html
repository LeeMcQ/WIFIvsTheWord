Privacy

Your prayers stay yours

Last updated: [DATE]

Wi-Fi vs The Word is a free ministry tool that helps families reduce digital distraction and grow in Scripture, prayer, and presence. We built it to protect your privacy by design. This explains, in plain language, what happens to your information, and is written to comply with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Responsible party: [Your registered entity / Pty Ltd], [address]
Information Officer: [name] — [email] — [phone]

The short version

  • Your spiritual life is yours. Almost everything stays on your device.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, run trackers, or build a profile of you.
  • Your prayer journal, confession entries, screen-time figures, streaks, family details, and reflections are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • The only time information leaves your device is when you choose to use the AI companion, or make a donation. Both are explained below.

What stays on your device (never sent to us)

  • Screen-time and app-usage figures (read from Android Usage Access / Apple Screen Time, which keep this data on your device).
  • Your daily goal, gentle-nudge settings, and "days of presence" streak.
  • Prayer journal entries, daily inventory, and anything written during a Confession — this is between you and God; it is never stored off-device or transmitted, and is not saved at all unless you choose to keep a journal note.
  • Your recovery "Journey" progress and trigger reflections (time of day plus a feeling you tap). Never linked to your identity, never leaves the phone.
  • Your nominated accountability partner's name and contact — used only to open WhatsApp or your email app on your device when you tap to send a message.
  • Your approximate location — used only to calculate sunset for the Digital Sabbath, computed on your device.
  • Your household / family member names and covenant.

The AI companion (optional)

If you choose to chat with the companion, the text of your messages and a small amount of non-identifying context (such as your screen-time total and goal for the day, and your streak count) are sent over a secure connection to our processing service and on to an AI provider to generate a reply.

  • We do not send your name, contacts, location, journal, or confession text.
  • Messages are processed to produce a reply and are not stored by us.
  • The AI provider may process the message on servers outside South Africa. Where that provider is in a country without comparable data-protection law, this is a cross-border transfer under POPIA section 72; by sending a message you consent to it. Every other feature works fully offline if you prefer everything stay on your device.
  • Please don't include personal identifiers or others' personal information in companion messages.

Donations (optional)

Giving is handled entirely by PayFast, a South African payment processor. Your payment details are entered on PayFast's secure pages and governed by PayFast's privacy policy. We never see or store your card or banking details.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
  • No analytics that track you across apps or build behavioural profiles.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal information for marketing.

Children and families

This is a family app, suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from children. Family features store names you enter only on your own device, and parents control all settings.

Security & retention

Information on your device is protected by your device's own security; the companion connection is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Because we hold almost no data ourselves, there is very little to breach. On-device information remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not retain companion message content after a reply is generated.

Your POPIA rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold (in practice, none that identifies you), to correct or delete it, and to object to processing. Since your data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly by editing or clearing data in the app, or by uninstalling. For anything else, contact the Information Officer above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the date above will change and significant updates will be noted in the app. Questions: [name] · [email] · [postal address].
