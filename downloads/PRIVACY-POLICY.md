# Privacy Policy — Wi-Fi vs The Word

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Wi-Fi vs The Word ("the app", "we") is a free ministry tool that helps families
reduce digital distraction and grow in Scripture, prayer, and presence. We built
it to protect your privacy by design. This policy explains, in plain language,
what happens to your information. It is written to comply with South Africa's
Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## The short version
- Your spiritual life is yours. Almost everything stays **on your device**.
- We do **not** sell your data, show ads, run trackers, or build a profile of you.
- Your prayer journal, confession entries, screen-time figures, streaks, family
  details, and reflections are stored **only on your device** and are never sent
  to us.
- The **only** time information leaves your device is when you choose to use the
  AI "Shepherd", or make a donation. Both are explained below.

## What stays on your device (never sent to us)
- Screen-time and app-usage figures (read from Android Usage Access / Apple
  Screen Time, which keep this data on your device).
- Your daily goal, gentle-nudge settings, and "days of presence" streak.
- Prayer journal entries, daily inventory, and anything written during a
  Confession — this is between you and God; it is never stored off-device or
  transmitted, and is not saved at all unless you choose to keep a journal note.
- Your recovery "Journey" progress and trigger reflections (time of day + a
  feeling you tap). This is never linked to your identity and never leaves the phone.
- Your nominated accountability partner's name and contact, used only to open
  WhatsApp or your email app **on your device** when you tap to send a message.
- Your approximate location, used only to calculate sunset for the Digital
  Sabbath. It is computed on your device and not sent to us or stored remotely.
- Your household/family member names and covenant.

## The AI "Shepherd" (optional)
If you choose to chat with the Shepherd, the **text of your messages** and a small
amount of **non-identifying context** (e.g. your screen-time total and goal for
the day, your streak count) are sent over a secure connection to our processing
service and on to an AI provider to generate a reply.
- We do **not** send your name, contacts, location, journal, or confession text.
- Messages are processed to produce a reply and are **not stored** by us.
- The AI provider may process the message on servers **outside South Africa**.
  Where that provider is in a country without comparable data-protection law,
  this constitutes a cross-border transfer under POPIA section 72; by sending a
  message you consent to this transfer. You can simply not use the Shepherd if
  you prefer everything stay on your device — every other feature works offline.
- **Please don't include personal identifiers or others' personal information in
  Shepherd messages.**

## Donations (optional)
Giving is handled entirely by **PayFast**, a South African payment processor. If
you donate, your payment details are entered on PayFast's secure pages and are
governed by PayFast's privacy policy. We never see or store your card or banking
details.

## What we do NOT do
- No advertising, ad networks, or ad identifiers.
- No analytics SDKs that track you across apps or build behavioural profiles.
- No selling, renting, or sharing of personal information for marketing.

## Children and families
This is a family app and is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal
information from children. Family features store names you enter only on your own
device. Parents control all settings.

## Security
Information held on your device is protected by your device's own security. The
Shepherd connection uses encryption in transit (HTTPS). Because we hold almost no
data ourselves, there is very little for anyone to breach.

## Retention
On-device information remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not
retain Shepherd message content after a reply is generated.

## Your POPIA rights
You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold (in practice, none
that identifies you), to correct or delete it, and to object to processing. Since
your data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly by
editing or clearing data in the app, or uninstalling. For anything else, contact
the Information Officer above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information
Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above will change and significant updates will
be noted in the app.
